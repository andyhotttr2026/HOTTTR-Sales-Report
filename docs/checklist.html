What to say

Openers

Short, and not a question. Under 20 characters gets a reply 66% of the time; 40–80 characters gets 17%. Openers with a question mark get 30% replies, without 43% — and twice as many of those fans go on to buy. Make a statement they'll want to answer.

Change a word every time. Two fans must never get the same line in a shift. Never mention that they just subscribed — "didn't expect you to sub right now" got 42% where "Heyy" got 86%.

Every offer to the same fan goes higher than the last. Fans whose prices climbed every time bought at 63%. Where a price repeated or dropped, 46%.

Don't price before three replies. Offers sent earlier convert at 23%; at ten or more replies, 45%.

Yasmine stops at $100 — re-sell at $50–100 instead. Emma Storm VIP and Fox Heart have no ceiling.

When they say no

A no is almost never about the number. Work out which one you've got.

"I can't afford it right now" They want it, the amount is too big in one go. Split it, don't shrink it.
→ "You can do it in two parts — try $75 and $75 and I'll send that one again unlocked 😘" This closed a fan who said he has trouble over $100.
"Can you do it for $30?" They're negotiating, which means they're buying. Counter with more, not less.
→ "$34.99? With a soap 🥵" Dropping to $30 teaches them to haggle every time.
"Not tonight" / "maybe later" Soft no. Don't accept it, don't push the same thing. Ask for their number.
→ "How much can you do right now, lovely?" Turns a vague no into a figure you can work with.
"I've got to go" / "I'm at work" Time, not money. Use their own clock.
→ "Can I show you something in that 10 minutes?" A fan had ten minutes of his break left and this got the sale moving.
"$200 is mental" The number scared them, not the idea. Drop a tier, agree with them first.
→ "Lol I get it. If 200 is mental to you, how about the 29 over here? ☝️" Arguing loses the chat.
"It wasn't what you said it was" A complaint, not an objection. Take it, don't debate it.
→ "You're right, that's on me — let me make it up to you." A fan explained exactly why he'd stopped buying and got argued with. He didn't come back.

Never go below a price they already refused. Change the item, hold the number. If you genuinely need to come down, ask your manager — 10% maximum, never twice on the same fan.

Don't do these

Don't offer before three replies.

Don't send the same 40-character message to more than 15 fans.

Don't message a fan who has ignored three messages.

Don't put a big spender in a mass message.

Don't sell below $10.

Don't let a fan ask "how much" and answer "up to you".
